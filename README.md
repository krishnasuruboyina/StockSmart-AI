# StockSmart AI 📦
### AI-Based Intelligent Inventory & Automated Purchase Request Generation System

## 📌 Project Overview
StockSmart AI is an AI-powered inventory management system designed to manage stock efficiently and predict future product requirements using Machine Learning. It analyzes historical sales data to forecast demand, prevent stock shortages, reduce overstocking, and automate the purchase process.

## ✨ Key Features

### 👨‍💼 Admin
- Manage company, employees, and suppliers.
- Add, update, and manage inventory products.
- Upload inventory and historical sales data using Excel/CSV files.
- Generate AI-based demand forecasts.
- Approve or reject employee purchase requests.
- Monitor purchase orders and confirm product deliveries.
- Update inventory only after confirming received quantities.

### 👩‍💻 Employee
- View inventory and available stock.
- Generate demand forecasts.
- Create purchase requests with quantity and reason.
- Track purchase request approval status.
- Receive approval and rejection notifications.

### 🏭 Supplier
- Login using supplier credentials.
- View assigned purchase orders.
- Accept or reject purchase orders.
- Mark accepted orders as dispatched.
- Track delivery status.

## 🔄 Project Workflow
1. Admin registers the company and creates employee and supplier accounts.
2. Admin adds products or uploads inventory data.
3. Historical sales data is uploaded for demand analysis.
4. Machine Learning models predict future product demand.
5. Employees generate purchase requests when additional stock is required.
6. Admin reviews and approves or rejects requests.
7. Approved requests generate purchase orders for suppliers.
8. Suppliers accept orders and dispatch products.
9. Admin confirms delivery and updates inventory based on the received quantity.
